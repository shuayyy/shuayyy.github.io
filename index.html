Mohamed Shuaib Nasurudeen

I am an M.S. student in Robotics Engineering at WPI, advised by Prof. Constantinos Chamzas.

My research focuses on humanoid loco-manipulation — enabling humanoid robots to interact with and maneuver wheeled objects. I develop approaches that integrate reinforcement learning, learned dynamics models, and motion planning for reliable whole-body control. More broadly, I work at the intersection of classical robotics and learning-based methods, with interests spanning model-based control, world models, and sim-to-real transfer.

I am currently seeking opportunities in humanoid robotics and robot learning.
