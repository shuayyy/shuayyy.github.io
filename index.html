Mohamed Shuaib Nasurudeen

I am an M.S. student in Robotics Engineering at WPI, advised by Prof. Constantinos Chamzas.

I work on humanoid loco-manipulation: making humanoids maneuver objects that push back, like carts and wheelchairs, through dynamic and learned models. I also work on LfD: learning from human demonstrations. My approaches use reinforcement learning, learned dynamics models, and constrained motion planning for whole-body control. More broadly, I am interested in how classical and learning-based methods suit different layers of a humanoid, from System 0 to System 1.
