Humanoid-Tugger

Developing a whole-body reinforcement-learning system that enables a humanoid robot to grasp and transport wheeled objects while maintaining stable locomotion and hand contact.

View video All projects

Humanoid-Tugger trains a single Unitree G1 policy for the full task — walking, grasping, and cart transport as one whole-body behavior rather than separate control problems. The system is still in simulation development.

My Work

Training environment, contacts, and reward redesign.

  • Built the PPO training environment in Isaac Lab covering walking, torso stabilization, dual-arm reaching, grasping, and cart transport.
  • Designed observations and rewards for gait tracking, palm and finger contact, handle alignment, cart position and orientation tracking, and whole-body stability.
  • Integrated Dex3 hands, contact-force sensing, inverse-kinematics-based pre-grasp initialization, and randomized cart dynamics.
  • Debugged failure modes including hopping, toe-standing, torso rotation, unstable contact, and reward exploitation through video analysis and reward redesign.
  • Evaluating the policy on straight-line cart transport before integrating learned cart dynamics and kinodynamic planning.
