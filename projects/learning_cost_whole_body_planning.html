Learning Cost-Driven Whole-Body Planning

Developing a whole-body planning framework that learns motion costs from retargeted human demonstrations and uses them to guide constrained trajectory optimization.

View demonstration All projects

This project explores how retargeted human demonstrations can teach a whole-body planner which postures and redundancy choices are more natural, then use that learned cost as guidance inside constrained trajectory optimization.

My Work

Learned guidance for constrained trajectory optimization.

  • Integrated learned costs with joint limits, collision constraints, task-space objectives, and trajectory smoothness.
  • Designed the planner to remain close to a reference motion while correcting infeasible or unsafe configurations.
  • Investigating how learned global guidance can reduce local minima, posture inconsistency, and abrupt redundancy changes.
  • Evaluating the framework on humanoid reaching and manipulation motions.
