Humanoid AMP-PPO

Built a whole-body reinforcement-learning framework for coordinated humanoid locomotion and dual-arm behaviors using PPO and human motion-capture priors.

View code View videos

A single policy controls the lower body, torso, and arms while human motion-capture data provides a motion prior for gait and posture. The goal was to train coordinated full-body behavior instead of isolating locomotion and manipulation into separate control problems.

My Work

Training stack, rewards, and evaluation.

  • Implemented the whole-body observation space, action space, PPO training pipeline, and AMP discriminator.
  • Combined task rewards with motion-prior rewards for locomotion, balance, posture, and dual-arm coordination.
  • Designed reward functions, curricula, termination conditions, observation noise, and domain randomization for stable policy training.
  • Analyzed policy videos and checkpoints to debug reward exploitation, unstable gait, excessive torso motion, and coordination failures.
